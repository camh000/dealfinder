Listings spotted at 12–20% off — just under the surfacing bar — are recorded as a control cohort: never notified, never shown in the feed, excluded from the model's training. If they end up winning about as often as the real deals, the 20% bar is too strict and we're leaving deals on the table. If they lose much more often, the bar is earning its keep.
