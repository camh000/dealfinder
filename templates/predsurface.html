Prediction-surfacing experiment

could we surface deals on predicted margin, not current discount? ← outcomes

Today a listing is surfaced when its current price is ≥20% under median. This experiment asks the other question: among the deals below that bar, the model flags the ones it predicts will still close ≥10% under median (using the outcome-calibrated snipe premium, not the current price). Those are recorded but never notified. If they win about as often as the live feed — and beat the sub-threshold deals the model didn't flag — then the prediction model discriminates well enough to surface deals on predicted margin.
