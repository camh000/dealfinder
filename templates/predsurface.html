Prediction-surfacing experiment

could we surface deals on predicted margin, not current discount? ← outcomes

Today a listing is surfaced when its current price is ≥20% under median. This experiment asks the other question: forget the current discount — the model flags every deal it is confident (≥75%) will close ≥10% under median. That confidence isn't a point guess: it's the share of the cohort's real past outcomes (final ÷ spotted price) that would land at/under the target, Wilson-adjusted for thin samples — so it folds in the model's bias and how noisy each cohort is. Those are recorded but never notified. If they win clearly more often than the deals the model was not confident about — the set this rule would skip — then the prediction discriminates well enough to surface on predicted margin alone.
