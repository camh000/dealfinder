BIN watcher (admin)

The scraper sweeps newly-listed Buy-It-Now items between full scrapes. You choose what gets pushed by creating watches on the Buy It Now page — browse with the filters you want, hit "Watch this", and matching finds notify your recipient. These admin settings just control the sweep itself (below); changes apply within a minute, no restart.

The discovery floor: the sweep only looks at finds this far under market, so a watch's threshold can't go below it.

Deal notifications

Push notifications go to each enabled recipient's Home Assistant, filtered to the categories they've ticked. Auction deals are sent once per deal when first spotted — and only when the prediction says the discount survives the bidding. BIN finds are sent the moment they're seen.
